# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Programme staff of the MAC-Egypt programme (administrators and field coordinators) and the
trainers who run technician training sessions. Staff work long admin sessions on laptops in the
programme office; field coordinators and trainers often work on tablets at workshops and training
venues. Trainers (TrainerViewer role) see and edit only their own sessions. Their jobs: register
and survey vehicle air-conditioning workshops, review scores and selection, record equipment
deliveries, and keep training records (sessions, participants, pre/post scores, photos) accurate.

## Product Purpose

MAC-Egypt is the digital assessment and monitoring platform for the vehicle air-conditioning (MAC)
servicing sector in Egypt, run for the UNIDO / NOU / EED programme. It replaces paper forms and
spreadsheets with one auditable record: workshop surveys, machine scoring, selection runs,
equipment handover, training records and reports. Success means every figure traces to a database
record, field data entry is fast and correct in Arabic, and programme decisions rest on data the
team trusts.

## Brand Personality

Calm, institutional, precise. The voice is plain, respectful and exact, in Arabic first and English
second. The interface should feel like a dependable government-programme tool: quiet confidence,
no hype, nothing that competes with the data.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS marketing gloss: hero banners, glowing gradients, upbeat filler copy.
- Decorative gradients, glassmorphism and ornamental color.
- AI-looking dashboards: hero-metric templates, identical card grids, neon accents.
- English-first layouts that break in RTL: hardcoded left/right, unmirrored icons, mixed-direction
  numbers rendered out of order.

## Design Principles

- Arabic first: every screen is designed in RTL and verified in LTR, never the reverse.
- Data is the hero: density with calm, one accent per region, status always carries a text label.
- Traceable by default: every number drills down to its record; show timestamps and audit context.
- Fast, forgiving data entry: inline over modal, keep context, offer undo instead of confirmation.
- Human decisions: the system suggests and records; people decide.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard access with visible focus, labelled controls, screen-reader announcements
for async results, 44px touch targets for tablet use in the field, reduced-motion support, and
status never conveyed by color alone. Western digits for data, with Arabic-Indic digit input
accepted and normalised.
